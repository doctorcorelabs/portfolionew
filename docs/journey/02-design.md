# 02 - Journey design: a winding annotated route (dark section)

Design only. No site file is edited by this document. The section
it designs replaces the current generic method explainer in
`src/sections/Manifesto.tsx` (the dark #111 block titled "From
question to tool" with four numbered rows). The replacement tells
the same story as a journey instead of a method lecture: how a
medical student who started by writing essays ended up building
tools. The spine it draws from is `docs/journey/01-spine.md`
(stations S1 to S30); every station below cites its spine number,
and no station carries a fact the spine does not record.

## 1. Core concept: one route, five year bends, three branch lines

A single winding route runs down a dark #111 section, from the
start of medical school in 2021 to the first tools in 2026. The
route is a dashed guide line with a blue progress thread that
draws along it as the visitor scrolls. Five large year nodes mark
the bends (2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026). Between bends, small
station nodes carry one recorded fact each, in plain callout text.
Award clusters appear as medal dots grouped beside their year
node, never as number cards. Research work leaves the main path
on three short branch lines (publications, research projects,
tools), so the route stays readable while the record stays
complete. The section title is "The route so far." with the mono
kicker "Journey". Nothing on the route is a KPI: counts appear
only as small mono legend text, never as large display numbers.

## 2. Station map (spine numbers in brackets)

The route groups the 30 spine stations into year bends. Inside a
bend, stations are ordered as the spine orders them, and each one
gets one callout of at most two short lines plus a mono date or
period label where the spine records one.

## 3. Desktop layout

- Section: background #111, full-bleed dark block, padding 130px
  top and bottom, 24px side padding. Inner container 1160px,
  centered. The existing slow blue drift blob behind the content
  may stay at very low opacity, but it must sit behind the route
  (z-index below the SVG) and must never reduce the contrast of
  any callout.
- Route canvas: one SVG spanning the container width, with its
  height set by the content (about 2400px to 2800px on desktop; the
  implementer sets the exact height from the station count, not
  from a fixed guess). The path is a winding S-curve: it enters at
  the top center, then bends alternately left and right so each
  year bend lands on the opposite side from the previous one. Bend
  amplitude is about 240px from the center line, so the route reads
  as a journey across the section, not as a straight timeline with
  decoration pasted on.
- Year nodes: 20px solid blue (#0859D4) discs with a 2px white
  ring, centered on the path. The year itself is set in JetBrains
  Mono 13px uppercase, letterspaced, in #FAFAFA, placed on the
  outside of the bend (the side away from the next bend), never
  overlapping the path.
- Station nodes: 10px discs, white fill with a 2px blue ring, on
  the path. The DaivanLabs hinge node [S20] is 14px with a blue
  ring and a white center, the only station node larger than the
  rest. The terminus "Now" node is a 20px blue ring with a dark
  center.
- Callouts: text blocks placed on alternating sides of the path,
  one per station, max width 340px. Each callout has a mono label
  line (12px, #98989C, uppercase: the station type, for example
  "Award", "Publication", "Role", "Talk") and a body line (17px,
  1.65 line height, #D8D8D8, Inter). Callouts connect to their
  node with a short 1px hairline leader (rgba white 0.25), at most
  48px long, so no callout floats unattached. Callouts on the same
  side are vertically spaced at least 120px apart, so text never
  collides even when a bend holds six stations.
- Section heading: Inter Display 700, clamp 48px to 72px, color
  #FAFAFA set explicitly on the heading element itself (never
  inherited: the current section's near-invisible title is the
  bug this rule prevents). Kicker: JetBrains Mono 12px uppercase,
  #98989C. Intro line (one sentence, max width 640px, 18px,
  #D8D8D8): "Essays first, then papers, then a lab, then tools."
  Closing margin note in mono 13px #98989C under the terminus:
  the recorded aggregate in plain text (29 awards recorded, 6 gold
  medals, 12 international items), never as display numbers.

## 4. Medal dots: award clusters, not KPI cards

Award clusters are the only place counts become visible marks, and
they are dots, not cards, not strips, not big numbers.

- Encoding (fixed legend, shown once in mono 12px #98989C beside
  the first cluster): solid blue dot = gold medal or 1st place;
  blue ring dot on dark = silver medal or 2nd place; small grey
  dot = bronze medal or 3rd place; open square = travel grant or
  special award. Dot sizes: solid 10px, ring 10px, grey 8px,
  square 10px. Dots are aria-hidden decoration; the cluster label
  beside them carries the same information in text, so nothing is
  conveyed by color or size alone.
- Placement: dots cluster in a loose arc of at most two rows beside
  their year node, on the outside of the bend, with 8px gaps. The
  2025 cluster (5 solid, 3 ring, 1 square for the travel grant,
  with the IIF Special Award noted in the cluster label) is the
  largest on the route. The 2023 cluster (1 solid for the essay
  1st place, 1 grey for the IRPOCO bronze, 1 ring for the RJJ 2nd
  place) sits beside the 2023 bend.
- Rule: a dot never stands for an award the spine does not record,
  and a cluster never shows a total the spine does not support. If
  a year has no awards, it gets no dots, not an empty cluster.

## 5. Research branches off the main path

Three branch lines leave the main path at a shallow angle (about
30 degrees) as 1px dashed spurs in rgba white 0.35. Each branch
ends in short leaf labels, not cards. Leaves are mono 12px
#D8D8D8 with a 6px blue square bullet; leaf text is a short topic
label, and the full verbatim publication title lives on the
research detail page the leaf links to.

- Publications branch (leaves the 2024 bend): four leaves for the
  2024 systematic reviews (gut axis Indonesian version, gut axis
  English version, colonoscopy computer-aided detection, RAAS and
  hypertension) plus one leaf for the 2025 Amomum cardamomum paper
  that joins the branch from the 2025 bend. No journal name, DOI,
  volume, or author list on any leaf.
- Research branch (leaves the 2025 bend): leaves for the PCOS
  dietary impacts review, the two Bisphenol A systematic review
  grants, and the two in silico drug discovery collaborations. The
  three undated leaves carry the "year not recorded" tag in mono.
- Tools branch (leaves the 2026 bend): three leaves, one per tool
  or project (FAERS poster with N = 6,601 only, StrokeSense and
  StrokeSense AI Lab with the honesty note, cerclage Table 1 with
  no result numbers). The tools branch is drawn with a slightly
  heavier spur (2px, solid blue at 0.5 opacity) because it is the
  destination of the whole route.
- Certifications branch (leaves the 2024 bend, opposite side from
  the publications branch): one leaf row of provider dots, not 20
  leaves. Four provider groups as mono text (WHO 2, UNITAR 1,
  Johns Hopkins 3, other Coursera providers 14) beside a row of 20
  small grey dots (6px, 6px gaps). No credential ID, expiry date,
  or grade anywhere.

## 6. Motion: path draw, station pops, stagger

All motion is scroll-linked or scroll-triggered, runs once, and
never replays on scroll up except the progress thread itself,
which reverses with scroll like the quote paint does.

- Path draw: the dashed guide line (rgba white 0.18, 2px, dash
  pattern 2px dash with 8px gap) is fully visible from the start,
  so the route is never a mystery the visitor must scroll to
  discover. Over it, a blue progress thread (#0859D4, 2px) draws
  along the same geometry as scroll progress advances. Progress is
  computed from the section's own passage through the viewport
  (0 when the section top hits the viewport bottom, 1 when the
  section bottom leaves the viewport top), via a single
  rAF-throttled scroll listener in the Quote pattern. Progress is
  reversible: scrolling up undraws the thread. The thread uses the
  SVG pathLength technique (pathLength 100, stroke-dasharray 100,
  stroke-dashoffset from 100 to 0); the dashed guide is a separate
  static path, so the draw technique never fights the dash
  pattern.
- Station pops: when the thread reaches a station's path
  position, that station's node pops from scale 0.5 to 1 over
  0.55s with the overshoot curve cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1),
  then its callout fades and rises 14px over 0.4s with the site
  ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Stations inside one year
  bend stagger by 120ms in spine order. Medal dots pop with the
  same overshoot curve at 80ms stagger per dot, after their year
  node. Branch leaves fade and rise 0.4s after their parent
  station pops, in branch order.
- Year nodes: pop with the same overshoot curve at 0.55s when the
  thread reaches them, and the year label fades in over 0.4s right
  after. The DaivanLabs hinge node gets one extra beat: a blue
  ring pulse (a 2px ring expanding from the node and fading over
  0.8s, once only) when it pops, because it is the hinge of the
  route.
- No other motion runs in the section. In particular: no card
  hover lift (there are no cards), no marquee, no looping ambient
  animation except the pre-existing faint drift blob if it is
  kept, and no scroll pinning of any kind.

## 7. Mobile fallback: one vertical route

Below 1023px the winding path is replaced by a single vertical
route, and every alternating-side rule is dropped.

- Geometry: one straight dashed guide line down the left side at
  left 20px, full section height, with the same blue progress
  thread drawing along it on scroll. Year nodes (16px) and
  station nodes (8px) sit on the line. All callouts sit to the
  right of the line in one column (padding-left 56px), max width
  none, in spine order. No callout ever sits on the left of the
  line, so nothing is clipped by the viewport edge.
- Branches collapse into the column: branch leaves become
  indented rows (padding-left 24px, 6px blue square bullets) under
  their parent station callout, in the same order as desktop.
  Medal dots become a single row under their year label. The
  undated spur becomes a final block headed "Year not recorded"
  at the bottom of the column.
- Motion on mobile is the same system at smaller sizes: thread
  draw on scroll, node pops with the same overshoot curve,
  stagger 100ms per station (slightly tighter than desktop).
  Section padding drops to 80px top and bottom, 24px sides.

## 8. Reduced motion: full route, no draw, no pops

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, the entire route renders
statically and completely on first paint. The guide line, the
progress thread at full length, every year node, station node,
callout, medal dot, branch leaf, and the terminus are all fully
visible with opacity 1 and no transform. No scroll listener runs,
no draw animation runs, no pop runs, and the hinge ring pulse is
omitted. Section height is the natural content height in all
modes: there is no pinned wrapper, no extra scroll runway, and no
inner scroll container, so the page has exactly one scrollbar.

## 9. Tokens, type, and contrast (hard rules)

- Palette: section background #111 only. Text: headings #FAFAFA,
  body #D8D8D8, muted mono #98989C, hairlines rgba white 0.15 to
  0.25. One blue only: #0859D4, used for year nodes, the progress
  thread, node rings, branch bullets, and the hinge pulse. No
  other accent color appears, including for medal dots (gold,
  silver, and bronze are encoded by fill and ring style per
  section 4, never by gold, silver, or bronze colors).
- Type: Inter Display for the section title and callout bodies
  (callout body weight 400, never bold except the station name
  inside the callout, which is bold); JetBrains Mono for the
  kicker, year labels, station type labels, legend, and the
  closing note. No other font appears.
- Contrast: every text element carries its own explicit color on
  #111 (the current section's near-invisible title happened
  because a heading relied on inherited color; that failure mode
  is designed out here). Body #D8D8D8 on #111 and muted #98989C
  on #111 are the only muted pairings, and muted text is never
  used below 12px.
- SVG: the route, nodes, dots, and branches are one inline SVG
  with aria-hidden on all decorative marks and real text (not
  SVG text) for every callout, label, and legend, so the content
  stays selectable, searchable, and readable without the graphic.

## 10. Explicitly forbidden

- Symmetric card grids: stations are never laid out as equal
  cards in rows and columns. The route is the layout.
- Vanity KPI strips: no row of large numbers, no count-up
  animation, no "29 awards" hero number. Aggregates appear only as
  small mono text in the closing note and the cluster legend.
- Generic line icons: no icon font, no outline pictograms for
  stations. Nodes, dots, and square bullets are the only marks.
- Invented numbers: no year, count, effect value, score, or
  percentage that the spine does not record. In particular: no
  FAERS effect value, no cerclage result number, no StrokeSense
  score or classification, no journal name, DOI, volume, author
  list, credential ID, expiry date, or grade, no phone number, and
  no home area.
- Scroll-lock: the section never pins, traps, or hijacks scroll.
  The visitor scrolls the page normally; the thread only reflects
  that scroll.
- Custom cursor: the system cursor is kept everywhere. The route
  is not interactive beyond links on branch leaves and the
  terminus status line.
- Marquee: nothing in the section loops horizontally or scrolls
  by itself. The only continuous motion allowed is the
  pre-existing faint drift blob, if kept, and it must not move any
  text.
